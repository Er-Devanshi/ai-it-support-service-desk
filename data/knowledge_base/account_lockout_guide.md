# Account Lockout Resolution Guide

## Overview
This guide helps employees resolve account lockouts and regain access quickly.

## Account Lockout Causes

- Multiple failed login attempts (typically 5+ within 15 minutes).
- Expired or incorrect credentials entered multiple times.
- Device date/time misalignment causing authentication token issues.
- Compromised password (security lockout).
- Synchronization issues during password changes.

## Self-Service Unlock Steps

### Option 1: Wait for Auto-Unlock
- Corporate accounts automatically unlock after 15-30 minutes of inactivity.
- This is the safest option if you can wait.
- Use this time to verify your credentials are correct before attempting login again.

### Option 2: Self-Service Portal Unlock
- Steps:
  1. Open the IT Self-Service Portal in a web browser.
  2. Select "Account Locked" under "Unlock My Account".
  3. Provide your email address and answer security questions.
  4. Your account will be unlocked immediately.
  5. Retry your login with the correct credentials.

### Option 3: Contact IT Helpdesk
- For urgent access needs, contact IT Support directly.
- Have your employee ID and registered email address ready.
- Helpdesk can unlock your account within 5 minutes.

## Steps After Unlocking

- Verify that you are using the correct username (typically firstname.lastname or employee ID).
- Confirm your password is entered correctly (note Caps Lock state).
- Check that your Caps Lock is OFF (common login mistake).
- Ensure your device date and time are correct.
- If using MFA, ensure your authenticator device is synchronized.

## Preventing Repeated Lockouts

- Write down your username and password in a secure password manager (e.g., Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password, Bitwarden).
- Test your login credentials on a non-critical system before using them for critical access.
- If you regularly mistype your password, consider resetting it to something you can type reliably.
- Enable biometric authentication (Windows Hello, fingerprint) if available to avoid password entry errors.

## Suspicious Lockout Pattern

- If you're being locked out despite entering correct credentials, your account may be compromised.
- Steps:
  1. Contact IT Security immediately.
  2. Do not attempt further login attempts.
  3. IT will audit your account for unauthorized access.
  4. You may be required to reset your password in the presence of an IT representative.

## Emergency Access

- If you are locked out and unable to use self-service tools:
  1. Call the IT Helpdesk at the published extension.
  2. Provide your employee ID and answer security questions.
  3. Helpdesk will unlock your account and guide you through re-authentication.
  4. Request a temporary password reset if needed.
