# Device Setup and General IT Troubleshooting Guide

## Overview
This guide covers setting up new work devices and troubleshooting general issues.

## Setting Up a New Work Device

**Step-by-step setup process:**

1. **Power on and complete the initial setup.** Turn on the device and follow the first-time setup wizard. Create or use your corporate account username and password if prompted.

2. **Join the corporate domain (Windows devices).** During setup or after logon, you may be prompted to join the corporate domain. Answer "Yes" and enter your corporate credentials.

3. **Connect to the corporate network.** Go to your network settings and select the official corporate Wi-Fi network. For wired connection, plug an Ethernet cable into the network port.

4. **Download and install required software.** Check the IT Self-Service Portal or ask your manager for a list of approved software and tools for your role. Download installers only from official sources.

5. **Set up email and calendar.** Open your email application and add your corporate email account. When prompted, enter your username and password.

6. **Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) for your account.** Go to the IT Self-Service Portal → Security → Set up MFA. Follow the steps to register an authenticator app or phone number.

7. **Install antivirus and security software.** If not already installed, download and install your company's approved antivirus from the IT portal. Ensure it is enabled and set to auto-update.

8. **Back up your data to cloud storage.** Set up automatic backup for your important files using your company's approved cloud storage service.

9. **Test access to key resources.** Verify you can access shared network folders, email, company intranet, and line-of-business applications. If you cannot access something, submit a ticket to IT immediately.

10. **Change your default password.** Go to Control Panel (Windows) or System Preferences (macOS) and change your password to something strong with uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.

**When to contact IT:** If you get stuck at any step or encounter errors, contact IT with the error message and which step you are on.

## Something Is Broken and Urgent: General Troubleshooting

**Step-by-step response:**

1. **Restart your computer.** Close all applications, perform a full shutdown (not sleep), wait 10 seconds, and power back on. Wait 2–3 minutes for the computer to fully start.

2. **Restart your network connection.** If network-related, disconnect from Wi-Fi or unplug the Ethernet cable, wait 10 seconds, and reconnect.

3. **Gather information about the problem:**
   - What is broken or not working (specific application, feature, device, etc.)
   - When it started (this morning, after an update, etc.)
   - What error message you see (write it down exactly if possible)
   - How many people are affected (just you, your team, the whole office)
   - What have you already tried

4. **Check if others have the same problem.** Ask nearby colleagues or your team lead if they are experiencing the same issue. If multiple people are affected, this indicates a system-wide problem.

5. **Check the IT Self-Service Portal or company intranet for status updates.** Sometimes IT posts notices about known issues or service outages.

6. **Look for an error message or status indicator.** Many applications show error codes or specific messages. Take a screenshot or write down the exact error.

7. **Verify your internet connection is working.** Open a web browser and try accessing a website. If the web works but a specific application does not, it is likely application-specific.

8. **If working remotely, check your VPN connection.** Ensure your VPN client shows "Connected". Reconnect if it shows "Disconnected" or "Connecting".

**How to submit a ticket or contact IT:**

1. **For immediate urgent issues:** Call the IT Helpdesk at the emergency number (check your company intranet or contact directory).

2. **For standard issues requiring support:** Submit a ticket through the IT Self-Service Portal:
   - Select "Create a New Ticket" or "Report an Issue"
   - Select the category that best matches your issue (Hardware, Software, Network, etc.)
   - Provide a clear title
   - In the description, include: what is broken, when it started, any error messages, what you have tried, and how many people are affected
   - Set the priority: Critical (cannot work at all), High (major feature not working), Medium (inconvenient but have workaround), Low (minor)

3. **Attach screenshots if available.** Include photos or screenshots of error messages.

4. **Provide your contact information.** Provide a phone number and email where IT can reach you.

**What IT will do:**

- Acknowledge receipt of your ticket within 15–30 minutes
- Prioritize based on urgency and business impact
- Contact you with follow-up questions or troubleshooting steps
- Provide a solution, workaround, or escalation to specialist teams
- Keep you updated on progress until resolved

**Important notes:**

- Do not attempt to uninstall system software or drivers unless IT instructs you to do so
- Do not share your password, even with IT staff
- If you receive an email or message asking for sensitive information (password, PIN, credit card), mark it as phishing and report it to IT Security
